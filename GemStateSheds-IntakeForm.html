How the Process Works
▼
Step 1 — Connect. We'll reach out to discuss the process. Can be done in-person or entirely over the phone.
Step 2 — Contract. Once your info is collected, a contract is written and sent for review and signing. Down payment is processed after your signature.
Step 3 — Payment. We accept cash, check, or card — no added fees for card transactions.
Step 4 — Delivery. Our team coordinates a delivery window that works for your schedule.
Step 5 — Move In. Our crew sets it on your site. Welcome to the family!
About Rent-to-Own
▼
No credit check — approval is based on identity verification, not credit score.
Flexible terms — 36, 48, or 60 month options. First payment due 30 days from purchase date.
No pre-payment penalty — pay it off early anytime and save on the remaining payments.
RTO Assurance — optional protection plan covers damage, theft, and more.
What Makes Our Buildings Stand Out
▼
LP SmartSide siding — engineered for moisture, impact, and pest resistance. Comes with a 50-year warranty.
Treated floor system — pressure-treated skids and floor joists resist ground moisture and rot.
Fully customizable — windows, doors, colors, insulation, lofts, and more.
Delivery & Site Information
▼
Site Prep: No special foundation is required, although gravel pads or concrete slabs are recommended for long-term stability. We deliver and level on treated skids and provide treated wood blocks up to 18″. If you prefer concrete blocks, simply have them on-site before delivery.
Fence or Gate Access: No problem — we offer custom on-site builds in most areas. Contact us for options if your space is tight.
Permits: Requirements vary by location. Customers are responsible for checking zoning, setbacks, and HOA regulations with city or county offices. We can provide building specs if needed for your application.
